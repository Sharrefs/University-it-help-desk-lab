Knowledge Base Article: Student Portal Access Issue

Issue:
Students may report that they cannot access the university student portal.

Symptoms:
- Student is connected to Wi-Fi.
- Student has normal Internet connectivity.
- Main university website is accessible.
- Student portal is inaccessible.
- Multiple students may experience the same issue.
- Issue may occur across multiple campus locations and devices.

Troubleshooting:
1. Verify that the student's device is connected to the network.
2. Verify that the device has an IP address and default gateway.
3. Test connectivity to the local gateway.
4. Test Internet connectivity.
5. Test DNS resolution.
6. Ask whether other users and devices are experiencing the same issue.
7. Determine whether other university websites/services are working.

Resolution:
If multiple users and devices are affected while general Internet connectivity is working, the issue may be a university service outage. Escalate the incident to the appropriate IT application/services team.

Important:
Do not make unnecessary changes to the user's network configuration when evidence indicates that the user's device and network connection are functioning normally.
